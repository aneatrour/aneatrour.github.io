I'm Anna Neatrour, Head of Digital Library Services at the Marriott Library, University of Utah. I've been a librarian for a looooooooong time. I started out as an Instruction Librarian and have worked on a variety of digital projects including the Western Soundscape Archive, the Western Waters Digital Library, and the Mountain West Digital Library. In my current role, I manage librarians and staff developing descriptive metadata, digitization, digital preservation, and the Utah Digital Newspapers project.
